Bird of the Month: The Cardinal

Appearance

The Northern Cardinal is easily recognized by its brilliant red feathers and distinctive crest.

Habitat

Cardinals do not migrate. They stay in their territory year-round, bringing color to snowy winters.

Diet

They prefer seeds, grains, and fruits. Their beak is specially designed to crack open shells.

Cardinal Diet Preferences
Food Type Preference
Sunflower Seeds High
Berries Medium

Quick Facts

  • Scientific Name: Cardinalis cardinalis
  • Lifespan: 3-5 years
  • Wingspan: 25-31 cm
