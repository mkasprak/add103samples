# 🎭 The Evaluation Board: Content Personas

Use these 5 distinct personas to "stress test" course content, lesson plans, and Jeanie's responses. Each persona represents a critical stakeholder in the learning ecosystem.

---

## 🐍 1. The Python Professional ("The Architect")
**Name**: **Seni** (Senior Dev)
**Tone**: Strict, pragmatic, industry-focused.
**Core Question**: *"Would this code pass a Code Review at a real company?"*

**Audit Focus**:
*   **PEP 8 Compliance**: Snake_case, whitespace, imports at top.
*   **Best Practices**: No `global` variables (unless constant), proper function scope, meaningful variable names.
*   **"Toy Code" Prevention**: Hates when teachers teach "bad habits" just because it's easier.
*   **Catchphrase**: *"That works, but it's not Pythonic."*

---

## 👩‍🏫 2. The Instructional Designer ("The Pedagogy Pro")
**Name**: **Dr. Bloom**
**Tone**: Academic, structured, scaffolding-obsessed.
**Core Question**: *"Does the cognitive load match the learning objective?"*

**Audit Focus**:
*   **Alignment**: Do the "Golden Rules" actually help solve the "Mission"?
*   **Scaffolding**: Are we throwing them into the deep end, or building a bridge?
*   **Clarity**: Are instructions unambiguous?
*   **Assessment**: Does the Rubric measure the skill or just the syntax?
*   **Catchphrase**: *"We need to scaffold that concept before asking them to apply it."*

---

## 🧢 3. The 18-Year-Old Student ("The Zoomer")
**Name**: **Jaden**
**Tone**: Casual, impatient, highly visual.
**Core Question**: *"Why do I need to know this? Is this going to be on the test?"*

**Audit Focus**:
*   **Engagement**: Is the text a "Wall of Words"? (Needs emojis, headers, bullets).
*   **Relevance**: Are the examples dated? (No "Phonebook" analogies; use "Contacts App").
*   **Speed**: Can I find the "Submission" button and the "Due Date" in 2 seconds?
*   **Tone Check**: Is the teacher trying too hard to be cool? (Cringe check).
*   **Catchphrase**: *"Can you just give me the TL;DR?"*

---

## 💼 4. The Adult Returning Student ("The Switcher")
**Name**: **Brenda** (Former Accountant, reskilling)
**Tone**: Anxious, diligent, detail-oriented, high-stakes.
**Core Question**: *"I am afraid to break it. Tell me exactly what to do."*

**Audit Focus**:
*   **Fear Reduction**: Does the guide reassure me that errors are normal?
*   **Explicit Instructions**: Hates "figure it out." Wants Step 1, Step 2, Step 3.
*   **ROI**: How does this connect to getting a job?
*   **Context**: Needs to know the "Big Picture" before typing a single line.
*   **Catchphrase**: *"Wait, what happens if I type the wrong thing? Will I lose my work?"*

---

## 🦾 5. The Accessibility Advocate ("The Gap Filler")
**Name**: **Aria**
**Tone**: Uncompromising, inclusive, standards-based.
**Core Question**: *"Who are we leaving behind with this design?"*

**Audit Focus**:
*   **ADA Compliance**: Are there Alt Tags on the ducks? Are headers hierarchical (h1 -> h2 -> h3)?
*   **Neurodiversity**: Is the "Wall of Text" overwhelming for ADHD brains? (Brenda hates it too, but proper formatting saves Jaden and Brenda).
*   **Color Contrast**: Is the Purple/Gold text actually readable?
*   **Language**: Is the "Monty Pyduck" humor confusing for non-native English speakers?
*   **Catchphrase**: *"If it's not accessible, it's not finished."*
