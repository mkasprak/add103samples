👋 Overview

The web isn't just text. HTML5 introduced native support for audio and video without needing plugins like Flash (RIP). Today we will build a custom media player and learn how to use background videos effectively.

🦆 Ducktor Merry Quacksalot says:
Always include controls on <video> and <audio> elements. Without it, mobile users cannot play your media at all. Accessibility and usability are the same thing.

🎯 The Mission

Create a "Media Lounge" page with Audio and Video elements.

  • Requirement 1: Embed an audio file with controls.
  • Requirement 2: Embed a video file with a custom poster image.
  • Requirement 3: Create a silent, autoplaying background video header.

💻 Code Sample

<!-- AUDIO PLAYER -->
<audio controls>
    <source src="podcast.mp3" type="audio/mpeg">
    Your browser does not support the audio tag.
</audio>

<!-- VIDEO PLAYER -->
<video controls poster="movie-poster.jpg" width="600">
    <source src="movie.mp4" type="video/mp4">
    Your browser does not support the video tag.
</video>

<!-- BACKGROUND VIDEO (Silent/Loop) -->
<video autoplay muted loop playsinline class="bg-video">
    <source src="background.mp4" type="video/mp4">
</video>

Detailed Example: Accessible Media Section with Captions

<section aria-labelledby="media-title">
    <h2 id="media-title">Media Lounge</h2>

    <!-- Audio with fallback text -->
    <audio controls>
        <source src="audio/interview.mp3" type="audio/mpeg">
        Your browser does not support embedded audio.
    </audio>

    <!-- Video with poster + captions track for accessibility -->
    <video controls poster="images/interview-poster.jpg" width="720">
        <source src="video/interview.mp4" type="video/mp4">
        <track src="video/interview-captions.vtt" kind="captions" srclang="en" label="English" default>
        Your browser does not support embedded video.
    </video>
</section>

🦆 Dig Deeper: Autoplay Policies

Browsers (especially Chrome and Safari) generally block video from autoclaving unless it is muted. Always include the muted attribute if you want your video to start automatically.
