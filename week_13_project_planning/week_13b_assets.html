🦆 Module 13B: Gathering Supplies

Content First

⏱️ Time: 30m Study / 90m Project | 🏆 Points: 20 | 📅 Due: Sunday 11:59PM

👋 Overview

You cannot design without content. Before we write a single line of code for the final project, we need to gather our raw materials: Images, Body Copy (Text), Icons, and a Color Palette.

🦆 Ducktor Merry Quacksalot says:
Your folder structure IS your architecture. Create it intentionally: images/, css/, js/ — lowercase, no spaces, no special characters. Clean naming signals a clean, professional codebase.

🎯 The Mission

Create a "Design System" folder structure with your assets.

  • Requirement 1: Collect at least 5 high-quality images (Unsplash/Pexels or your own).
  • Requirement 2: Write the actual text for your Homepage (don't use Lorem Ipsum!).
  • Requirement 3: Define your Color Palette (Primary, Secondary, Accent colors in Hex).

🦆 Dig Deeper: Copyright

Never just steal images from Google Images. Use royalty-free sites like Unsplash, Pexels, or Pixabay. If you use a copyrighted image in a real project, you can get sued.
