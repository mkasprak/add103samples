👋 Overview

Welcome to the Final Project. For the next 4 weeks, you will build a complete, multi-page website from scratch. This week is all about planning. "Measure twice, cut once."

🦆 Ducktor Merry Quacksalot says:
A solid planning document saves hours of aimless building. Spend 30 minutes planning now and you’ll save 3 hours of rework later. Planning is not procrastination — it’s professionalism.

🎯 The Mission

Submit your Site Proposal and Wireframes.

  • Requirement 1: Write a 1-paragraph "Pitch" (Who is the site for? What is the goal?).
  • Requirement 2: List the 4 pages you will build (e.g. Home, About, Gallery, Contact).
  • Requirement 3: Draw a Wireframe for your Homepage (Hand-drawn or Figma).

1) Home (hero + value statement + CTA)

4) Contact (form + social links)

🦆 Dig Deeper: Low-Fidelity vs High-Fidelity

We are doing Low-Fidelity wireframes today. We don't care about colors or specific fonts yet. We care about LAYOUT. Where does the button go? How big is the hero image? Don't waste time on polish yet.
