Quality Assurance

⏱️ Time: 30m Study / 45m Project | 🏆 Points: 10 | 📅 Due: Sunday 11:59PM

👋 Overview

It's not enough for code to "work." It must be fast, accessible, and follow best practices. Today we use Google's industry-standard tool, Lighthouse, to grade our own work.

🎯 The Mission

Run a Lighthouse Audit on your live site and fix the issues.

  • Requirement 1: Achieve a score of 90+ in Accessibility.
  • Requirement 2: Achieve a score of 90+ in Best Practices.
  • Requirement 3: Submit a report of what you fixed (e.g., added alt text, increased contrast).

🦆 Dig Deeper: SEO

Lighthouse also checks SEO (Search Engine Optimization). While we focus on Accessibility today, getting a 100 in SEO ensures Google ranks your page higher in search results. The #1 rule? Have a unique <title> and <meta name="description"> for every page.
