Reply (By Sunday):

🗣 Use the Professional Critique Script:
  1. "I like..." — Name something specific that works well. (Not "looks cool" — be precise. "I like that the nav collapses cleanly on mobile.")
  2. "I wish..." — Identify a specific gap. ("I wish the body font size were larger for readability.")
  3. "What if..." — Suggest a concrete improvement. ("What if you added a hover state to those cards so they feel clickable?")
  • Review at least 2 Classmates.
  • Use the "I like / I wish / What if" framework above — one full pass per peer.
  • Must Use Vocab: Use words like "White Space", "Contrast", "Responsiveness", "Flex Alignment" in your critique. "Looks good" is not a gradeable critique.

📘 Detailed Example: Constructive Critique Template

Glow:
Your navigation hierarchy is clear and your spacing creates strong readability.

Grow:
At 375px width, the hero heading wraps awkwardly and overlaps the button.
Try reducing heading size in a media query:

@media (max-width: 600px) {
    .hero h1 { font-size: 1.8rem; }
}

Glow:
Your color contrast is strong, and link hover states improve usability.
