🗣️ Online Students: The Virtual Showcase

Discussion Requirement

Post (By Wednesday):

  • Upload a screenshot of your Homepage.
  • Post the GitHub Pages Link (Live URL) to your site.
  • Self-Reflection: Write 1 paragraph on "The Hardest Bug I Squashed." How did you solve it?

Reply (By Sunday):

  • Review at least 2 Classmates.
  • Use the "Sandwich Method": Compliment -> Critique -> Compliment.
  • Must Use Vocab: Use words like "White Space", "Contrast", "Responsiveness", "Flex Alignment" in your critique. "Looks good" is not a gradeable critique.

🏫 Live Students: The Gallery Walk

Instructions:
