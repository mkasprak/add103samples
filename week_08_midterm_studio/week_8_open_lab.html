🚑 Emergency Room: Common Issues

Stuck? specific solutions to the most common Midterm bugs:

Issue 1: "My Grid is collapsing/exploding!"

The Fix: Check your image sizes. If you put a 4000px image into a grid cell without setting width: 100% in CSS, it will force the column to expand infinitely. Always constrain your media!

Issue 2: "My Mobile View looks weird."

The Fix: Did you write Mobile First? If you wrote complex Desktop grid code without a media query, your phone is trying to render 4 columns. Wrap your complex layout code in @media (min-width: 768px) { ... }.

Issue 3: "My Navigation Links are vertical."

The Fix: Remember to apply display: flex to your `nav ul` (the list container), not the `nav` itself. Then use gap: 20px to space them out.
