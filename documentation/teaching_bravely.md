# Teaching Bravely Online: Principles to Practice

**Inspiring Innovation, Connection, and High-Quality Learning**

*Included as a core guiding document for the ADD 103 Course Rewrite.*

---

### Principle 1: Encourages Contact Between Learners and Instructor
**Focus:** Instructor Presence & Connection
*   **Goal:** Intentionally *show up* for students in ways that build trust, clarity, and momentum.
*   **Application:** Ensure every module has a personal note, video, or direct channel for feedback.

### Principle 2: Reciprocity and Cooperation Among Learners
**Focus:** Learning Community & Peer Connection
*   **Goal:** Bravely design opportunities for students to learn *with* and *from* one another.
*   **Application:** Peer reviews, code sharing, and "debug together" forums.

### Principle 3: Uses Active Learning Techniques
**Focus:** Engagement & Meaningful Learning
*   **Goal:** Students actively creating, applying, or reflecting on knowledge.
*   **Application:** "Teach by Example" (Watch One, Do One), interactive coding labs.

### Principle 4: Gives Prompt Feedback
**Focus:** Guidance, Growth & Improvement
*   **Goal:** Feedback helps students move forward with confidence.
*   **Application:** clear rubrics, automated checks, and specific milestone reviews.

### Principle 5: Emphasizes Time on Task
**Focus:** Clarity, Pacing & Student Effort
*   **Goal:** Clearly communicate expectations for effort, pacing, and flexibility.
*   **Application:** "Estimated Time" badges on assignments, clear due dates.

### Principle 6: Communicates High Expectations
**Focus:** Rigor, Transparency & Quality
*   **Goal:** Make high expectations visible and attainable.
*   **Application:** Showing rigorous "Look & Learn" examples of what quality code looks like.

### Principle 7: Respects Diverse Talents and Ways of Learning
**Focus:** Equity, Inclusion & Flexibility
*   **Goal:** Invite multiple ways for students to engage and demonstrate learning.
*   **Application:** Multiple modes of submission (Video, Code, Written), rigorous Accessibility standards.
